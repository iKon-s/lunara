Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers before your visit.

1. Do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend reserving for groups of 5 or more.
2. What are your opening hours?
We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
3. Do you have outdoor seating?
Yes. Outdoor seating is first come, first served, and can be requested when you reserve.
4. Can I study or work in the café?
Absolutely. Bring your books and laptop. There is no rush here.
5. Do you offer small gatherings?
Yes. Our tables suit small gatherings of friends and family. Please reserve ahead.
6. Where are you located?
On the Second Floor, Main Mall, SM Mall of Asia in Pasay City.
7. How can I contact you?
Email us at lunaracafe@lunaracafe.com or use the form on our Contact Us page.
